Contact us

Email: support@olsot.uz

We usually reply within 1–2 business days. To help us resolve your issue faster, include your app version, phone model, and a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app, go to "Sign up", and enter your phone number and a password. You can add or change your name later in your profile settings.

I forgot my password — what should I do?

Password reset is not yet available in the app. Email us at support@olsot.uz with the phone number linked to your account, and we will reset it once we have verified your identity. If you still know your password and simply want to change it, go to Profile → Change password.

How do I post a listing?

Sign in to your account and open the "add listing" section. Enter the title, description, price, category, and photos of your item. Your listing appears in the catalogue once it passes moderation.

What is not allowed to be listed?

Any goods or services prohibited by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan: weapons, drugs, forged documents, stolen goods, as well as any listing intended to deceive other users. Such listings are removed without prior warning.

I'm not receiving notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings (Settings → Apps → Ol-sot → Notifications). Notifications must also be enabled in your profile settings inside the app. Battery-saver mode can block notifications as well.

How do I report a scam or an inappropriate listing?

Send the listing link or a screenshot to support@olsot.uz. We review every report and block listings and accounts that break the rules. Ol-sot is a listings platform — the transaction itself happens between buyer and seller, so we recommend paying only after you have seen the item in person.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Your account and the data linked to it will be removed. This action cannot be undone. If you cannot sign in, email support@olsot.uz. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I change the app language?

You can switch between Uzbek, Russian, and English in your profile settings. Your choice is stored on your device.
